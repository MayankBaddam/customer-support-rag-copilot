# CloudDesk Subscription Plans

**Document ID:** KB-PLANS-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15  
**Status:** Active

## Plans

CloudDesk offers three fictional plans billed monthly or annually.

| Plan | Included seats | Storage | Integrations |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter | 5 | 10 GB | 3 |
| Team | 25 | 100 GB | 15 |
| Scale | 100 | 1 TB | Unlimited |

All plans include ticket collaboration, workspace search, audit events, and email support during business hours.

## Changing plans

A workspace owner can request an upgrade at any time. An upgrade takes effect immediately, and the billing adjustment is calculated for the remaining period.

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. Before downgrading, the workspace should reduce seats, storage, and integrations to fit the lower plan limits.

## Cancellation

A cancellation stops renewal but does not remove access immediately. The workspace remains available through the end of its paid period. CloudDesk does not promise a refund solely because a subscription was cancelled.

## Limits and review

Plan limits are measured per workspace. Support agents should confirm the current plan and usage before describing an upgrade or downgrade outcome. Pricing and promotional terms are intentionally omitted from this fictional knowledge base.
