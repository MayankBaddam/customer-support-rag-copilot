# CloudDesk Account Recovery

**Document ID:** KB-ACCOUNT-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15  
**Status:** Active

## Forgotten password

Use the **Forgot password** link on the CloudDesk sign-in page and follow the recovery message sent to the account's verified recovery address. Recovery messages expire after 30 minutes.

Support agents must not ask customers to share passwords, one-time codes, recovery links, or full payment details.

## Lost access to the recovery address

If a customer cannot access the verified recovery address, the request requires an identity review by an authorized workspace owner or CloudDesk support reviewer. Do not change the address based only on a chat request.

## Multi-factor authentication

A customer who loses a multi-factor device should use a previously saved recovery code. If no recovery code is available, the account requires the same identity review before multi-factor authentication can be reset.

## Suspicious access

If a customer reports an unfamiliar sign-in, advise them to change their password after recovery and review active sessions. Escalate suspected account takeover for security review. Do not disclose session identifiers or internal review details.

## Safe handling

Account recovery proves access through the approved recovery flow; support conversations alone do not prove account ownership.
