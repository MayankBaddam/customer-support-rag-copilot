# CloudDesk Refund Policy

**Document ID:** KB-REFUNDS-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15  
**Status:** Active

## Eligibility

CloudDesk customers may request a subscription refund within 30 calendar days of the initial purchase or a paid plan renewal. Refunds are reviewed against the account billing history and are not automatic approvals.

A refund request submitted after 30 days is outside the standard refund window. Support agents should explain the policy and escalate only when a documented billing error is present.

## Billing errors

If CloudDesk charged an account twice for the same billing period, the duplicate charge should be investigated and corrected. The support agent must verify the billing record before promising a refund.

## Process

Customers should contact CloudDesk support from inside the workspace and include the approximate charge date and plan name. Never request or record a full payment-card number, password, or authentication code.

Refunds, when approved, are returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider and is not controlled by CloudDesk.

## Scope

This policy applies to fictional CloudDesk subscriptions. It does not authorize support agents to approve refunds automatically or to make exceptions without the documented review process.
