# CloudDesk Data Retention

**Document ID:** KB-DATA-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15  
**Status:** Active

## Workspace data

Active workspace tickets and conversation records are retained while the workspace is active. Deleted records enter a 30-day deletion queue before permanent removal, subject to legal or security preservation requirements.

## Audit events

Security and administrative audit events are retained for 12 months. Audit events are read-only and are not removed when a ticket is closed.

## Backups

Encrypted service backups may retain deleted data for up to 35 days. Backup copies are not available for normal customer browsing or support export.

## Export and deletion requests

A workspace owner may request an export or deletion review through CloudDesk support. Support agents must verify the requester's authorization and must not promise a completion date before the request is reviewed.

## Scope

This fictional policy describes CloudDesk workspace data only. It does not define a legal privacy notice or replace a formal contract.
